High-Impact Corporate Invoicing

Standard ERP invoices often suffer from awkward margins, generic layouts, and lack of statutory wording required in international B2B commerce.

The cc_custom_invoice_report module delivers a high-definition QWeb PDF invoice template with standardized 12mm print margins, automated legal Amount in Words conversion across global currencies, and clean customer billing metadata.

Key Capabilities

Executive PDF Layout

Clean typography, itemized line tables, and crisp borders designed to represent your corporate brand professionally.

Legal Amount in Words

Automatically computes and renders total amounts in legal English wording for Dollars, Euros, Pounds, and Riyals/Halalas.

Optimized A4 Paperformat

Calibrated 12mm margin settings eliminate page clipping across standard office, thermal, and virtual PDF printers.
